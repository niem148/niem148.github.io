# Linksys SPNMX56 vs Plusnet Hub Two: Which Router Is Better?

Choosing the right router can make a huge difference to your home network, especially if you rely on stable Wi-Fi for work, streaming, gaming, or smart home devices. In this post, we compare the **Linksys SPNMX56**, a modern Wi-Fi 6 mesh-capable router, with the **Plusnet Hub Two**, the ISP-provided router used by thousands of UK households.

# Wi-Fi Standards: Wi-Fi 6 vs Wi-Fi 5

The SPNMX56 uses **Wi-Fi 6**, which offers:

- Better performance with many devices  
- Improved efficiency  
- Higher throughput  
- Lower latency  

The Plusnet Hub Two uses **Wi-Fi 5**, which is still reliable but lacks the modern optimisations found in Wi-Fi 6.

---

# Security: WPA3 vs WPA2

Security is one of the biggest differences.

## Linksys SPNMX56
- Supports **WPA3**, the latest Wi-Fi security standard  
- Stronger protection against password cracking  
- Better device onboarding  
- More future-proof  

## Plusnet Hub Two
- **WPA2 only**  
- Still secure, but older  
- No option to upgrade to WPA3  

If you care about encryption strength, the Linksys wins clearly.

---

# Real-World Performance

The SPNMX56 is designed for high-density environments:

- Multiple 4K streams  
- Gaming  
- Smart home devices  
- Mesh expansion  

The Plusnet Hub Two is stable and simple, but not built for heavy loads or large homes.

---

# Mesh Capability

The SPNMX56 can be expanded into a full mesh system, ideal for:

- Larger homes  
- Thick walls  
- Dead zones  

The Plusnet Hub Two has **no mesh support**.

---

# Which One Should You Choose?

## Choose the Linksys SPNMX56 if:
- You want Wi-Fi 6  
- You need stronger security (WPA3)  
- You plan to expand with mesh nodes  
- You have many devices  
- You want better long-term performance  

## Choose the Plusnet Hub Two if:
- You want simplicity  
- You’re on a basic Plusnet package  
- You don’t need mesh or advanced features  
- You prefer plug-and-play  

---

# Final Thoughts

For most modern households — especially those with multiple devices, smart home setups, or high-speed fibre — the **Linksys SPNMX56** is the clear winner. It offers better security, better performance, and better expandability.

However, the **Plusnet Hub Two** remains a solid, stable option for users who just want a simple router that works without configuration.
