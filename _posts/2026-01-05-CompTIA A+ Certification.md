# My CompTIA A+ Certification Journey

I’m excited to share that I’ve officially passed my **CompTIA A+ certification**, a key milestone in my transition into IT and technical support.

To prepare, I followed the **Udemy CompTIA A+ course by Mike Meyers**, which gave me a strong foundation and plenty of practical context. From there, I built a study workflow that helped me stay organised and target my weak areas effectively.

---

## How I Studied

### OneNote for Structured Notes  
I used OneNote to break down each domain, summarise topics, and track areas I needed to revisit.

![OneNote Notes](/assets/media/comptia_a_plus/Screenshot 2026-02-10 143922.png)  
*My OneNote structure for A+ revision.*

---

### Mock Tests + Anki Flashcards + Copilot Integration  
Alongside mock exams, I created Anki flashcards to reinforce key concepts.

A big boost came from **exporting all my Anki cards and importing them into Copilot**, along with my mock test results. This let me focus on the exact cards linked to my weaker areas, making my revision far more targeted and efficient.

![Anki Setup](/assets/media/comptia_a_plus/Screenshot 2026-02-10 144225.png)  
*My Anki deck used for spaced‑repetition revision.*

---

### Extra Practice: PC Building Simulator  
Outside of formal study, I also spent time with **PC Building Simulator**, which turned out to be a surprisingly useful way to reinforce hardware concepts, compatibility checks, and troubleshooting flow — all of which show up in the A+ exam.

![PC Building Simulator](/assets/media/comptia_a_plus/Screenshot 2026-02-10 144356)
